Q5 · PART 1 · THE KITCHEN
What year did Corning introduce the Pyrex bakeware that filled American kitchens?
Borosilicate glass, invented for railroad lanterns. Bessie Littleton baked the first pie in a battery jar in 1913. By 1940 every house had a piece. Grandma's bowls were never going to wear out.
