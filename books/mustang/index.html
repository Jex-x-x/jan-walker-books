Q2 · PART 1 · ORIGINS
On what date did the Mustang make its public debut at the New York World's Fair?
Press saw it April 14; the public April 17, 1964 — on sale the same day nationwide. Dealers were mobbed that weekend. Ford sold 418,000 in year one, a new- nameplate record for decades.
