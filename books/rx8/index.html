RX-8 Trivia & Fun Facts

A sports car with four doors, no pillar between them and an engine with no pistons. It sounds like a committee's compromise. A German engineer designed a rotary compressor in the 1920s, a motorcycle maker turned it into an engine, and a small Japanese company bet its future on it. Ninety verified questions across ten parts — the cockroach car and the condition for four doors, an exhaust moved to the side, the editions of four countries, and the only Le Mans win by a car without pistons.

Q77 · PART 9 · THE ONLY ROTARY AT LE MANS
What makes the 787B win at Le Mans in 1991 unique?
Only win by a car without pistons — it won on reliability and fuel economy, not outright speed. No other car without a piston engine has won the race since.
