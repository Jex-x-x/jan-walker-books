Q52 · PART 6 · THE 22B
How many 22B STIs did Subaru build in 1998, counting the prototypes?
400 for Japan, 16 for the UK, 5 for Australia, 3 prototypes. The Japanese allocation sold out in as little as 30 minutes. Built for Subaru's 40th birthday and a third straight constructors' cup.
