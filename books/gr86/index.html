86 & BRZ Trivia & Fun Facts

One company wanted a small, light, rear-drive sports car. Another had a flat-four engine and no appetite for building one alone. They said no to each other once, the project stopped for six months, and then they tried again. The result was sold under five names, at least two badges and a lot of colour options. Ninety verified questions across ten parts — why the engine is 86 millimetres wide in two directions, how the twins were told apart, and the second generation that came in yellow, green and orange.
