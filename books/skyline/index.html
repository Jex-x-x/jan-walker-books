Q15 · PART 5 · THE RB26DETT
Why is the RB26DETT exactly 2,568 cc and not a round 2.6 litres?
Group A multiplied turbo engines by 1.7. 2,568 x 1.7 lands just under the 4,500 cc class ceiling with the widest tyres allowed. A regulations document designed one of the great road engines.
