Q47 · PART 6 · PRODUCTION HELL
The car was meant to be called the Model E. It is not. Why?
The company applied for Model E in 2013. A rival opposed the filing, citing an earlier agreement, and the application was abandoned. The range was meant to spell S-E-X-Y; it reads S, 3, X, Y.
