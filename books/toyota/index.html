Q31 · PART 4 · GOES ANYWHERE
The first heavy four-wheel drive of 1951 wore a different name. Why was it renamed in 1954?
It went on sale in August 1951 as the Toyota Jeep BJ. Willys-Overland held the rights to that name, so in 1954 the vehicle was renamed Land Cruiser — a replacement that has outlived the argument by seventy years.
