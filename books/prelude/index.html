Prelude Trivia & Fun Facts

In 1987, a two-door coupe did something no production car had ever done before: it steered from all four wheels. No computer, no electric motor — just a mechanical system reacting to how far and how fast the driver turned the wheel. It stayed in production for twenty-three years before Honda quietly let it go in 2001, then brought the name back in 2026 as a manual-free hybrid that shares nothing mechanical with the original. Ninety verified questions across ten parts — the name Toyota trademarked first, the VTEC timeline most retellings get wrong, and the torque-vectoring trick almost nobody remembers.

What world first did the third-generation Prelude achieve in 1987?
First mass-production car with four-wheel steering — Honda’s own heritage materials call it the first steering-angle-sensing four-wheel-steering system on a production car, full stop.
