E30 Trivia & Fun Facts

The most famous car in this book has four cylinders, not six. BMW built about 2.3 million E30s between 1982 and 1994, and from one of them, made under a racing rule, it produced a road car that won the championships it was designed for. Ninety verified questions — the M3 and its homologation rule, Camshaft Paul and the S14 engine, the touring-car titles, and the tuner that became a manufacturer.

Q39 · PART 5 · FOUR CYLINDERS, SIX-CYLINDER GENES
What was Paul Rosche’s nickname, because of his early specialisation in camshaft calculation?
Camshaft Paul — in German, Nocken-Paul. Rosche ran BMW’s racing-engine development, led the S14, and retired in 1999 at the age of 65.
