Volkswagen says 13 million Transporters have been sold since 1950, in five countries. Q73 · PART 9 · THE CHICKEN TAX
What rate did the Chicken Tax put on imported light trucks?
25 per cent — introduced in January 1964 as retaliation for European tariffs on American chicken. The van counted as a light truck, so it was hit too.
