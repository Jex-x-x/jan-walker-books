MX-5 Miata Trivia & Fun Facts, Volume 2

The roadster that reinvented an entire segment gets a second volume of what the first one left out. In Japan it was sold under a different badge entirely. An electric prototype was built in 1993 with a regional power company and never reached a showroom, and a windscreen-less concept followed in 2009. A factory restoration programme for the very first car began in 2017. Ninety verified questions across ten parts — concepts that never left a trade-show stand, a fire that nearly ended a production run, the longest-running one-make race series recognised by Guinness World Records, and the cars built on its chassis under other names.
