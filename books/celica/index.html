Celica Trivia & Fun Facts

For sixteen years the Celica was a rally weapon. For the sixteen years either side of that, it was something much quieter. In 1995, FIA scrutineers found a hidden device in the turbo plumbing of Toyota’s works rally car — a spring-loaded restrictor plate that let more air reach the turbo than the rules allowed. FIA president Max Mosley called it the most ingenious thing he had seen in thirty years of motorsport, right before banning the team for a year. Ninety verified questions across ten parts — the secret name it shared with the Supra, three World Rally Championship titles, and the engine the final Celica shared with a Lotus Elise.

What was the full scope of Toyota Team Europe’s 1995 penalty?
Exclusion from 1995, plus a twelve-month suspension — the team lost its entire 1995 points total, then sat out all of 1996 too, the harshest penalty of the whole scandal.
