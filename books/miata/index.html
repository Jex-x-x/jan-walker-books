Q4 · PART 1 · NA GENERATION
What was the pop-up headlight actuator time from fully closed to fully open on a 1990 NA?
The NA's pop-up headlight motors were engineered for a 1.2-second full deploy. Faster than most competitors of the era. The assembly is now a JDM restoration pilgrimage part.
