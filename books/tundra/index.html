Q6 · PART 3 · 2ND GEN
In what model year did the Tundra's 3UR-FE 5.7L V8 first appear, and what horsepower did it debut at?
The Tundra 5.7L 3UR-FE launched in the 2007 model year at 381hp and 401 lb-ft. It ran nearly unchanged through 2021 — a 13-year production span that outlasted several competitor V8s.
