Z3 Trivia & Fun Facts

BMW paid nothing for the Z3’s part in a Bond film, and the placement turned into one of the most successful of 1995. The little roadster was built in South Carolina, not Germany, and a hundred replicas of the film car went through a Christmas catalogue. Ninety verified questions — the Z1 that came before it, the Spartanburg plant, the engine range from fours to the M Roadster, and the Coupe nicknamed the clown shoe.

How much did BMW pay for the placement of the Z3 in the film?
Nothing. No money changed hands: BMW tied the film to the car’s launch campaign instead, and it had script approval.
