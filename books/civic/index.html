Q10 · PART 2 · UNDER THE BONNET
How much power did the B16B in the EK9 Civic Type R produce?
185 PS at 8,200 rpm, from 1,595 cubic centimetres, without forced induction, in 1997. That is a little over 115 PS per litre — more than a contemporary Ferrari F355 managed. The torque figure was the price of it: 160 newton metres, arriving at 7,500 rpm, which is where most engines of the period had stopped pulling.
