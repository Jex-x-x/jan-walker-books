Q2 · PART 1 · A NAME BORROWED
In Spain and in most of Spanish-speaking America, the full-size model was sold under a different name. Which?
In Spanish slang pajero is a vulgar insult, and no dealer wanted to sell a car called that. Montero is a Spanish word for a huntsman, which kept the outdoor idea and lost the problem. When the smaller model arrived, it simply added a word to whichever name the local big brother carried.
