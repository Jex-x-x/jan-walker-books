Q10 · PART 2 · THE PEOPLE’S CAR
Who restarted Volkswagen production after the Second World War?
Wolfsburg fell in the British occupation zone. Major Ivan Hirst, a REME officer, had a surviving car painted green and presented to the army, which ordered 20,000 vehicles — enough to justify restarting the line in 1945. Heinrich Nordhoff took over in 1948 and ran the company for twenty years, but the plant was already running when he arrived.
