3000GT Trivia & Fun Facts

In Japan it was the GTO, a name borrowed from an Italian racing legend. In North America it was the 3000GT, and also the Stealth. It steered with all four wheels, had spoilers that moved by themselves and a twin-turbo V6, just as Japan’s economy began to soften. Then the money ran out and the gadgets were dropped one by one. Ninety verified questions across ten parts — the pace car that lost its job, the lightweight version that beat a Skyline, and a folding hardtop nobody had marketed since 1959.
