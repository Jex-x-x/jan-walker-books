Q1 · PART 1 · ORIGINS
Which head of state's investment triggered development of what became the G-Class?
Iran held ~18% of Daimler-Benz and ordered 20,000 trucks for border patrol. The Shah fell 16 days before the first G rolled off the line in 1979 — and the order died with the regime.
