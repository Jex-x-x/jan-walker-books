Inside the book

10 parts. 90 questions.

The MX-5 exists because a journalist would not let an idea go. Bob Hall, an American motoring writer who had moved into product planning at Mazda, spent years arguing for a small, light, rear-drive roadster at a company with no obvious reason to build one. The engineering target was not a rival but a car out of production since 1973: Hirai’s team studied the Lotus Elan closely enough to take one apart.
