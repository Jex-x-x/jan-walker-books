Inside the book

10 parts. 90 questions.

Some cars are famous under one name. This one spent thirty years changing its name at the border, and most of the people who drove it had no idea that a buyer two countries away knew the same machine by a different badge. It began as the smaller, lower, cheaper relative of a four-wheel drive that had made its reputation in the Sahara. It was meant for people who wanted that reputation without that size.
