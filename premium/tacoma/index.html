Inside the book

10 parts. 90 questions.

For nineteen years, Toyota sold a compact pickup in North America under a name so plain it barely qualified as one. From 1976 until the 1995 model year, the truck that the rest of the planet knew as the Hilux was sold in the United States and Canada as the Toyota Pickup, or on the window sticker, simply the Toyota Truck. It was a deliberate choice. Toyota wanted the North American truck to read as a local product rather than an import, and a name that said nothing at all said it very effectively.
