Inside the book

10 parts. 90 questions.

Most cars in this series exist because somebody found a gap in a market. The S2000 exists because a company had a birthday. Honda Motor Co. was incorporated on 24 September 1948. Fifty years later it was the largest engine manufacturer on earth by unit volume, it had just come out of a decade of Formula One dominance, and it built its money in family saloons and small SUVs. What it did to mark the anniversary was build a two-seat, front-engined, rear-wheel-drive roadster with no automatic gear‐ box, no glovebox, no turbocharger and no obvious commercial logic, and give it a four-cylinder engine that revved to nine thousand.
