The Mercedes-Benz G-Class is the boxy off-road wagon that a Shah ordered, a hand-assembly plant in Graz still builds, and Beverly Hills valets have quietly resented since 2007. Forty-seven years after the first W460 rolled off the line at Puchwerk in Austria on February 1st, 1979, the silhouette has es‐ sentially not changed. The doors still make a distinctive metallic thunk. On the combustion-engined models, three fully-locking differentials still have three separate dashboard buttons.
