Most performance cars are road cars that were later taken racing. The Impreza is the other way round. Subaru needed a car to win the World Rally Championship, built one, and then sold the public whatever number of cop‐ ies the rule book demanded. That is why a family saloon from a small Japanese manufacturer has bonnet vents, a gearbox with a mechanical centre differential and a boxer engine with forged internals — none of which a fam‐ ily needs, and all of which a rally car does.
