Inside the book

10 parts. 90 questions.

The Skyline is older than almost everyone who loves it. It arrived in April 1957, built not by Nissan but by a company called Fuji Precision Machinery, which had spent the previous few years renaming itself Prince — in honour of Akihito’s formal investiture as Crown Prince — and would change its mind about that name twice more before settling. The first Skyline was a 1.5- litre luxury saloon with sixty horsepower and a bench seat.
